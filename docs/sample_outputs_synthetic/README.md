# Sample outputs (SYNTHETIC data)

These figures show **what each output of the pipeline looks like**. They were generated by running the
whole pipeline on the fake dataset from `scripts/make_synthetic_dataset.py` (drawn ellipses, not X-rays),
with a small CNN. Every image carries a red banner saying so.

- They are **not** medical results and must **not** be copied into `images/` or quoted in your README.
- Your real proof comes from training on the Kaggle dataset, then running
  `python scripts/collect_proof_images.py`, which copies your own figures into `images/`.
